KAISA Advocacy Stories

The Transparency Mandate

At KAISA, the objective was to bridge the gap between government policy and public understanding. I engineered communication assets that gave the 'Right to Information' a credible and modern visual voice.

By standardizing the design language of civic advocacy, I helped elevate the initiative’s credibility, ensuring that governance messaging resonated as effectively in local counties as it did on national digital platforms.
