Empowering Remote Jobs

Overview

National initiative designed to equip youth with digital skills and connect them to online work opportunities.

Sponsored by Mastercard Foundation, KEPSA, eMobilis, and **Ministry of ICT**. Focused on standardizing the ecosystem through strategic visual alignment.

The Challenge

Traditional vs. Digital Perception

Many young people perceived employment primarily as traditional office work, making it difficult to communicate the legitimacy and potential of digital freelancing.

The Solution

Visual Credibility Systems

  • Visual Infographics: Simplifying complex economic concepts into relatable visuals.
  • Success Stories: Documenting real-life outcomes to build legitimacy and trust.
  • Scalable Templates: Enabling consistent partner communications across platforms.
