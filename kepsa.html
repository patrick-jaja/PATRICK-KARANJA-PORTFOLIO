KEPSA Digital Ecosystem

Building a unified communication architecture to consolidate Kenya's national AI and digital employment programs under one premium visual umbrella.

The Challenge

The Kenya Private Sector Alliance (KEPSA) was simultaneously running several high-profile national programs including Ajira Digital, KAISA AI Alliance, and DSEEAP. They needed a unified communication system that could sit above these individual programs, providing a premium, executive-level visual umbrella while allowing each sub-program to maintain its distinct identity and appeal to its specific demographic.

The Solution

I built a comprehensive ecosystem brand architecture. This involved designing overarching visual identity systems, directing all corporate photography and videography assets to ensure a consistent high-end look, producing executive summit documentation (including the KEPSA media book), and managing strategic event communications for national summits and forums.
