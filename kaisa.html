KAISA AI Alliance

Designing the visual identity and communication system for Kenya's first national AI Skills Coalition, partnered with Microsoft.

The Challenge

Kenya's first national AI Skills Coalition needed a credible, forward-looking visual identity and communication system. The challenge was uniting the government, private sector giants, and civil society around responsible AI adoption and skills development, and presenting this unified front to a national audience eager to understand the impact of AI on jobs.

The Solution

I designed the entire KAISA brand identity from the ground up. This extended into creating the AI Week event communications, producing high-end documentary films exploring AI's local impact, developing Microsoft co-branded materials, and managing all visual assets for the national launch of the AI alliance.
