Swvl Transit Expansion

Designing the bold brand roll-out that cemented Swvl's mass-market dominance across Kenyan transit corridors.

The Challenge

Swvl was rapidly expanding its tech-driven transit network in Kenya. However, breaking into the deeply entrenched local public transport ecosystem required a massive, unmissable brand presence. The challenge was making the Swvl brand hyper-visible both on the streets and in the digital space, specifically highlighting new features like the M-Pesa integration which was critical for local adoption.

The Solution

I executed a highly visible, multi-channel creative campaign. This included designing massive, eye-catching OOH bus wraps that turned the fleet into moving billboards, coupled with dynamic motion graphics campaigns to educate users on the new M-Pesa app integrations. The aesthetic was bold, fast, and distinctly tailored to the daily Kenyan commuter.
