The Economic Bridge

At KYEEAP, the challenge was to make bureaucratic employment programs feel personal and attainable. I engineered communication assets that simplified the journey from program application to real-world employment.

By standardizing the design language of youth opportunities, I ensured that every digital banner and print flyer carried the weight of government credibility while maintaining the energy of youth-centric culture.
